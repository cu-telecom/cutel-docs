# Electromagnetic Field 2026

[Electromagnetic Field](https://www.emfcamp.org/) describes itself as "a non-profit camping festival for those with an inquisitive mind or an interest in making things: hackers, artists, geeks, crafters, scientists, and engineers". It will take place in July 16–19th 2026 at Eastnor Castle in Herefordshire.
